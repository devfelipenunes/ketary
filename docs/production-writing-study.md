# Independent writing study and production roadmap

Thirty prompt-only tasks are in production-writing-tasks.json. They are not 30
completed programs, nor independent usability observations. Use a blinded
within-participant crossover, counterbalanced order and equal documentation,
time limits and tool access. Recruit experienced and new quantum programmers;
record success against a hidden independent oracle, semantic errors, time to
correct solution, debugging, confidence, native fallback and maintenance.
Exclude participant-identifying data from public artifacts. Report all failed
tasks. Separate agent study fixes model/version, seed where supported, prompt,
context, execution budget and first-attempt success. Do not infer human
productivity from authored code length.

## Qualification of mathematical research

test_production_approx_geometry.py contains 18 differential geometry cases
(analytic expectation Jacobian, finite-difference Hessian, exact single
occurrence state derivative and gauge-invariant QGT), 36 first-order
two-term Lie formula error-bound cases (operator norm), and 60 seeded
reversible circuit permutation/phase oracles. Hessian and QGT are research
calculations, not public APIs. The first-order bound is
|t|^2 ||[A,B]||/(2r) for the Hermitian two-term model, and the second-order
comparison is only an observed finite corpus. Do not generalize the bound
to arbitrary formulas or floating-point synthesis without error analysis.

## Platform qualification

A separate workflow installs the candidate in Linux, Windows and macOS on
Python 3.12, records actual resolved packages and runs selected mathematical
and signature tests. This is a smoke gate, not full platform compatibility.
Linux constraints are not imposed on other OSes. A failed platform is a
research finding; preserve the failure, do not declare supported until full
suite and packaging/dynamic qualification pass. No upload or release.
